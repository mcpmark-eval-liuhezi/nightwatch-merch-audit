## 1. Artwork record (Rijksmuseum collection, SK-C-5)

- **Official title:** Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'
  (English: Militia Company of District II under the Command of Captain Cocq, known as the 'Night Watch')
- **Object number:** SK-C-5
- **Dating:** 1642 (signed and dated on the canvas: 'Rembrandt f 1642')
- **Technique/Materials:** Oil on canvas
- **Maker:** Rembrandt van Rijn (1606–1669), Amsterdam
- **Career context:** Mid-career peak work — painted at the height of Rembrandt's Amsterdam success, midway between early history pieces (e.g. the allegorical Music Company, 1626) and late masterworks such as The Sampling Officials (1662) and The Jewish Bride (c. 1665–1669). It is the culmination of his 1630s portrait/civic-duty period.
